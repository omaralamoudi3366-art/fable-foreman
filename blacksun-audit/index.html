3. Is the evolution healthy?

The engine runs; the mutation is not producing fitter strategies. The daemons have been evolving continuously, and the scores inside the single-split test rose (Kuma 51.5 on Sep 28 to 77.5 on Oct 17). That rise did not survive walk-forward, which is the sign of a search fitting one data split rather than finding a durable edge.

From search volume to validated edge

Counts as last recorded. Bars are not to a common scale; each row is a stage of the same pipeline.

  • A units bug poisoned the search for weeks. All nine strategy-family genomes swept risk_per_trade_pct over 0.25–1.0, which the sizing code reads as 25%–100% of equity. Against the $500 per-trade cap, every trade breached, so no candidate could pass ruin-prevention. This was identified on Sep 14 as a cause of "1,690+ generations, 0 graduations". The session ended with the fix waiting for your approval, and the champion's ID on Oct 14 still contains risk_per_trade_pct-0p25. I could not confirm the fix was ever applied.
  • Two search loops are tapped out. Zeus's own mutation search is disabled; Kuma's cannot fix a sizing problem by tuning parameters.
  • The idea pipeline is empty. The discovery agent that is meant to feed new strategies made zero proposals in every run on Nov 4–6. The local model never called a tool, and three of four sources were blocked (GitHub 401, QuantConnect 202, Reddit 403).
  • Overfitting checks are not running. The deflated-Sharpe gate tests fail and the overfitting / PBO test files cannot be collected because scipy is missing from the daemon environment. These have been carried as "pre-existing, unrelated" failures since at least Nov 5.
  • One clean negative result. The liquidity-sweep reversal signal was tested three ways, found to have no edge, and dropped (Oct 8–14). That is the process working as intended.

6. Bottlenecks and where to go from each

1 · No strategy survives walk-forward

The live champion trades on a standard the system's own stricter test rejects. The decision "accept walk-forward or stay on single-split" has been open since Oct 17.

Go: keep SMC in paper or minimum size and treat walk-forward as the bar for anything risking evaluation capital. Stop tuning the four heads' parameters. Put the effort into the two candidates with real signal (ORB NY GBPUSD, Argus-Zeus) and into new ideas.

2 · Sizing units bug, fix unconfirmed

Go: open configs/fleet/genomes/*.yaml and check the risk_per_trade_pct ranges. If they still read 0.25–1.0, change them to 0.0025–0.01, add a load-time assertion that rejects any value above 0.02, and re-score the leaderboard. ORB NY GBPUSD may graduate on this alone.

3 · Too little data to validate anything

There is a 524-day gap in NQ one-minute history, and SMC produces about 7 trades a year. The Databento key has been waiting on you since Oct 16.

Go: create the Databento account ($125 free credit covers the backfill) and put the key in .env yourself. Then re-run walk-forward on all heads. For SMC, add instruments so the trade count supports a verdict.

4 · Overfitting guards switched off in practice

Go: install scipy in .venv-daemons, get the 6 uncollectable files and 7 failing tests green, and make deflated Sharpe and PBO part of the promotion gate. With 7,600+ generations of trials, this is the control that matters most.

5 · Nine audit findings left open on live-money controls

Flatten Now can silently do nothing and still show "cleared"; its PENDING state can stick; proposal approval has no lock; regime breakdown can hang a request.

Go: resume that session and finish high, then medium, then low, as you instructed on Nov 7. Do the two Flatten items before trusting the button.

6 · The idea pipeline produces nothing

Go: finish the A/B re-run, wire plan-then-run into the daemon, and replace the 14B local model in the judge seat if it still fails. Until then, feed candidates by hand from the reel research.

7 · One fragile PC runs everything

The live feed depends on a TradingView Desktop window with a specific chart pane open; it was down two days before anyone noticed. On Nov 4 the PC had 690 MB of 32 GB free while paper trading ran.

Go: alert on feed staleness through the Telegram bot that now works, add a watchdog-of-watchdogs check, and stop running research jobs on the trading machine during market hours.

8 · Work is spread thin and keeps hitting the session limit

Three sessions died mid-task on Nov 8. Up to three sessions commit to the same branch at once. Recent effort went to the dashboard, chart engine, journal and on-chain contracts while bottlenecks 1–4 sat.

Go: one strategy-critical stream at a time. Park the chart engine, journal and Sepolia deployment until a strategy passes walk-forward.

9 · Telegram bot token exposed

The bot token was pasted into chat in plain text several times and is stored in those transcripts.
